One author per commit, and it is the human at the machine. Do not add a
`Co-Authored-By:` line for an agent — Claude, Copilot, Warp, Oz or whatever
comes next — nor `Generated with Claude Code`, nor an `@anthropic.com`,
`@warp.dev` or `copilot@github.com` address, in commit messages, PR bodies or
changelogs. Every repo in this organisation had its history cleaned of those on
2026-08-21; do not reintroduce them.
